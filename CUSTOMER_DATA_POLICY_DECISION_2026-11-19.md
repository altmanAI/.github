## Decision and scope

**Approved direction:** Strict prohibition on customer-content training of general/customer-independent AI models; meaningful human authorization of consequential AI actions; evidence-backed, public-safe governance; immediate commencement of implementation work.

**Included:** policy text, verification questions, release conditions, reference to existing governance, future review and corrective steps.

**Excluded:** publication of private chat history, internal/private repository information, credentials, vendor contracts, customers' data; unsupported legal or security certification; assertion of Zoom partnership or endorsement; renaming historical AltmanAI Model 2.0 records; direct change to production services or provider settings.
